# GoatCounter

Follow these steps to configure GoatCounter for Metrix.

## Connecting to GoatCounter

1. Log in to your [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) site.
2. Create or copy an **API Key** from your GoatCounter settings, with permission to read statistics. Also allow reading site settings to use **All Time**, which reads the site’s first recorded hit.
3. In Metrix, create a Source and choose **GoatCounter**.
4. Enter your **Site URL** (e.g. `https://yoursite.goatcounter.com`).
5. Paste the **API Key**, save, and Connect.
